# No paid features, no premium tier, no feature gating — ever

The app will always be free with no paid tier. This is a founding promise, not a future option. No feature is gated behind payment. No export is locked. No caregiver slots are capped at a paywall. No extra Baby profiles require upgrading.

Donations are entirely optional and exist only to help cover hosting and maintenance costs. The donation link lives outside the core workflow and is never shown in context that implies pressure. Parent-facing copy reads: "Free. No ads. No paid tier. Donations optional."

This is recorded as an ADR because future contributors or forks may be tempted to introduce a freemium model. This decision forecloses that without explicit reconsideration.

## Consequences

Hosting and maintenance costs must be kept low enough that the app can run sustainably on donations or personal sponsorship. Firebase free tier should be sufficient for private use; public scale requires monitoring but not a paid product.
